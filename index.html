Cold-Weather Running Gear | Checklist

The clothing, shoes and accessories you need to brave the cold on your next run.

Cass da Costa | Published December 22, 2025

Running in the cold can be thrilling. The winter air keeps your body from overheating, and may make you faster. And a little outdoors time during a long winter can lift your spirits while giving you an additional boost of endorphins. The right kit is essential, though. Without insulated, moisture-wicking apparel, for example, your lingering sweat could lead to hypothermia. High quality cold-weather running gear will keep you exploring your favorite running routes all year long.

Here is a checklist to help you assemble the perfect cold-weather running kit, including recommendations for our favorite base layers, a traction device to keep you from slipping, and more.

Editor's note: If you have any chronic respiratory issues, you may want to avoid exercising outdoors in extremely cold weather, particularly sub-zero temperatures. This article is intended to be merely informative and should not be a substitute for actual medical advice.
